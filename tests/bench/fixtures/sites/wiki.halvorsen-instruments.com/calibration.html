TQ-40 calibration procedure (rev. F)

  1. Let the logger stabilise for 30 minutes at room temperature (20–25 °C).
  2. Immerse the probe in the 0.00 °C ice-point reference for 10 minutes; record the mean of 60 readings.
  3. Move the probe to the 70.00 °C dry-block reference for 15 minutes; record the mean of 60 readings.
  4. Compute offset = −(ice-point mean) and gain = 70.00 / (dry-block mean − ice-point mean).
  5. Write offset and gain with tqctl cal set --offset <o> --gain <g>; values must be within ±0.5 °C and 0.98–1.02.
  6. Out-of-range values: do not write them; tag the unit "RETURN" and open a ticket.
