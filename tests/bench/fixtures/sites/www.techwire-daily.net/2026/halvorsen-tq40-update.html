Halvorsen pushes TQ-40 firmware update

Published 2026-08-25

Halvorsen Instruments released firmware 3.4.2 for its TQ-40 thermal logger last week, on August 12, fixing a Wi-Fi reconnection bug. The company says the rate limit of its API doubles to 20 requests per second. Battery life is quoted at 18 months.

A 4.0 release with MQTT support is expected before the end of the year.
