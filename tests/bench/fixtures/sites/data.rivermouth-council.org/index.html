Rivermouth open data: cycling counters

Daily rider counts from four automatic counters, June 2026: bike-counts-2026-06.csv.

Columns: date, site, count, rain_mm, unit. Known issues: the North Gate counter reported in hundreds of riders for three days after a firmware change (unit column says so); the Mill Lane counter had an outage on one day (count left empty); the export job duplicated a few rows.
